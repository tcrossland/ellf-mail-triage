Sort this email by what kind of mail it is, to decide what to keep from an old archive. When two kinds seem to fit, take the first that fits in this order: anything about your job is WORK, whoever sent it, even an invoice or a booking, except automated mail from a work system, which is AUTOMATED; a person you know writing about your life outside work is PERSONAL; your own affairs with an organisation (a record of something you bought, booked, paid or signed, a decision or obligation such as a tax assessment, an appointment or a claim, or a person there answering you) is ADMIN; routine mail a system sends about your account or activity (codes, alerts, shipping updates, reminders, notices of new terms or privacy policies, activity on things you're involved in) is AUTOMATED; anything sent the same to many people, including mailing lists and releases of projects you only watch, is BULK. Mail from a person is WORK only when it is about your job. A List-Unsubscribe header points to BULK, but go by what the message is.
